Awk Console - FL Studio controller scripts + agent connector, by awk.

Not included: Arturia's "KeyLab Essential V2" FL Studio script (by Farès MEZDOUR / Arturia).
The installer copies it from your own FL Studio install or Arturia download and makes two tiny
edits (removes one import line; drops the '# name=' line of the entry file).

Ideas (no code) from z-midi-console and fls-pilot by zorroukk (MIT).
